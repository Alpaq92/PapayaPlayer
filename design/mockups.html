  • Colour sources: fixed, accent, custom list, gradient, hue shift, album-art colours.

Decided (2026-10-30)

  1. Controls style: both, minimal (01) and accent (02), switched in settings.
  2. Audio: the same control bar as video, with art, title and visualiser above it (03).
  3. Gradients: from Reaktor's CSS; the request is written up for Reaktor.
  4. Accent colour: papaya orange, with the guava-pink gradient.
  5. Playlist: a slide-out panel (03).
  6. Visualiser: bars by default; the other styles and colour sources selectable (04).
  7. Settings: a panel inside the main window (05).
