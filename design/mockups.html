  • A theme is a named preset: style + placement + colour source, kept in settings.
  • Colour sources: fixed, accent, custom list, gradient, hue shift, album-art colours.

To decide

  1. Controls style: minimal (01), accent (02), or both with a setting as in 05? Both costs little extra once the widgets exist.
  2. Gradients: draw them in our own widgets now, or ask for gradients in Reaktor's CSS (with the GPU-renderer and HDR requests already in REAKTOR-HDR.md)?
  3. Accent colour: papaya orange with a guava-pink gradient, as shown, or another?
  4. Audio screen: playlist always below (03), or a slide-out panel behind the list button?
  5. Visualiser: which style and colour source is the default, and does it also run behind video-less files in full screen?
  6. Settings: a separate window (05), or a full-height panel inside the main window?
